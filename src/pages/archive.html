Research archive

Forty years of occam, CSP and transputer research is spread across old university servers, the WoTUG site and personal archives. This page collects it in one searchable place, and it is still growing.

Papers

Key papers and manuals, chosen by hand, together with all 708 records from the WoTUG paper database: every occam User Group, WoTUG and Communicating Process Architectures conference from 1987 to 2013. Links go to the copies hosted by WoTUG.

    Collections & mirrors

    wotug.org is no longer maintained, but much of its static content is still online, including directories that are not linked from its front page. These are the most useful ones, along with mirrors elsewhere.

    Rebuilding the corpus

    The goal is a permanent, citable corpus of occam-related papers. It should not depend on any one university or society keeping a server running. The plan:

    1. Index. Catalogue every paper in the WoTUG paper database and the IPCA, with a stable ID, BibTeX and topic tags. Done for the WoTUG database: all 708 papers are indexed above, with abstracts where WoTUG has them (mostly from the late 1990s on). Topic tags are assigned automatically and could do with checking by hand.
    2. Mirror. Where rights allow, keep a copy of each PDF here and deposit it with the Internet Archive, so a dead link never loses a paper again.
    3. Connect. Link papers to the lessons, implementations and pages that build on them.

    If you hold proceedings, theses, Inmos technical notes or course notes that are not online, please get in touch.
