    Rebuilding the corpus

    The goal is a permanent, citable corpus of occam-related papers. It should not depend on any one university or society keeping a server running. The plan:

    1. Index. Catalogue every paper in the WoTUG paper database and the IPCA, with a stable ID, BibTeX and topic tags.
    2. Mirror. Where rights allow, keep a copy of each PDF here and deposit it with the Internet Archive, so a dead link never loses a paper again.
    3. Connect. Link papers to the lessons, implementations and pages that build on them.

    If you hold proceedings, theses, Inmos technical notes or course notes that are not online, please get in touch.
